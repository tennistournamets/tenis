Americano and Mexicano are the most popular social padel formats. The idea is simple: you play with different partners rather than one, and the winner is decided by total points scored, not by matches won. Everyone plays the same amount and the event fits a known time slot.

## Americano: the basic rules

1. **Partners change every round.** The schedule is built so that everyone partners everyone else at least once. For 8 players on 2 courts that is 7 rounds.
2. **Matches are played to a fixed number of points.** Usually 16, 24 or 32 points per match. Every rally counts; there are no games or sets.
3. **Serve changes every 4 points.** Each player serves 4 times in a row, then the serve passes on.
4. **Points go to the players.** If your pair wins 14–10, you and your partner each get 14 points and your opponents 10.
5. **The leaderboard is the sum of points.** The player with the most points across all rounds wins.

There is also **Team Americano**: pairs stay together, but points are counted the same way.

## Mexicano: what is different

Mexicano scores like Americano, but pairs are not fixed in advance: they are formed after every round from the leaderboard. On court 1 the 1st and 4th play the 2nd and 3rd, on court 2 the 5th and 8th play the 6th and 7th, and so on. Matches get closer and the strongest players meet each other.

## Tips for organisers

- **Plan the time.** A 24-point match takes about 15–20 minutes. Seven rounds take roughly two hours with breaks.
- **The number of players should divide by 4.** If it does not, some players sit out each round and receive the average score.
- **Announce the tie rule in advance.** For example head-to-head or the number of matches won.
- **Show the results to everyone.** A live leaderboard on phones or a screen by the courts keeps the suspense until the last round.

## Can you run Americano with Bracketa?

Today [Bracketa](/en) supports tournaments with fixed pairs: round robin, groups with a playoff, single and double elimination. If you play Team Americano or simply want every pair to play every other pair, choose round robin: the schedule and the table are built automatically and spectators follow the scores live.
